Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for supporting Reach Cares. Donations are gifts that we put to work in our projects, so they are generally not refundable. We do understand that mistakes happen, and this policy explains when and how we can help.

When you can ask for a refund

  • you were charged more than once for the same donation;
  • you sent a different amount than you intended;
  • the payment was made without your permission.

How to ask for a refund

Send us a message through the Contact page within 7 days of your donation and include:

  • your full name and the email address you used when donating;
  • the date and amount of the donation;
  • the reference ID of the payment;
  • a short explanation of what went wrong.

How refunds are handled

We review every request and will let you know the outcome by email. Approved refunds are returned to the e-wallet or bank account the donation came from. How long it takes for the money to arrive depends on your payment provider.

Annual donations

If you chose to donate annually and want to stop, let us know through the Contact page. Stopping future donations does not refund donations you have already made.

Questions

If you are unsure whether your situation is covered, reach us through the Contact page and we will help.
