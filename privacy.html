Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Reach Cares collects through this website, why we collect it, and what your rights are. We handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us:

Personal information collected through this website
Form What you give us What we use it for
Information Required?
Donation form Salutation Optional To address you correctly
First name and surname Required To record your donation and match it to your payment
Suffix Optional To record your name correctly
Email address Required To send you a confirmation or contact you about your donation
Donation amount and frequency Required To record your donation
Payment reference ID Required To match your donation to your payment
Contact form Topic and question or comment Required To reply to your questions and messages
Phone number Optional To reach you about your message
Email address Required To reply to your message
Not collected: account, card, or login details. Payments are made in your own e-wallet or banking app.

The membership application is a Google Form. What you enter there is collected through Google Forms, not through this website.

How we use your information

  • to record your donation and match it to your payment;
  • to send you a confirmation or contact you about your donation;
  • to reply to your questions and messages;
  • to keep the records we are required to keep.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing your information

We share personal information only when it is needed to run our work, for example with service providers that help us operate the website, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above or as required by law. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Keeping it safe

Access to personal information is limited to the people in our team who need it for their work, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from loss or misuse.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal information is used;
  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • have incorrect information corrected;
  • object to our use of your information or withdraw your consent;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, reach us through the Contact page.

Children

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us personal information or making a donation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
