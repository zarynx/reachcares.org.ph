About REACH Cares

↓
What is REACH Cares?
REACH Cares is a youth-serving, community-based organization in Rizal, Nueva Ecija. It is accredited by the Local Youth Development Office of the Municipality of Rizal, Nueva Ecija and registered under the National Youth Commission's Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP).
How did REACH Cares start?
It began on March 13, 2024 as Project REACH (Regalo ng Edukasyon, Alaga, at CHansa), a school project to help the Aeta community of Villa Labrador, Rizal, Nueva Ecija. It has grown into a youth-led movement known as REACH Cares. You can read more on the Who We Are page.

Becoming a member

↓
How can I become a member?
Go to the Be one of us page and fill in the application form.
Why should I join?
You serve communities and make a real difference, help the youth and students through meaningful programs, gain leadership and volunteer experience, become part of a purpose-driven and compassionate team, and grow personally while helping others in need.

Donating

↓
How do I donate?
Use the donation form on the Take Action page. Choose how often and how much you want to give, fill in your contact details, scan the QR code with your e-wallet or banking app, and enter the reference ID of your payment.
Can I choose my own amount?
Yes. Pick one of the suggested amounts or tick "Choose your own amount" and enter any amount you like. You can give one time or annually.
What is the reference ID?
It is the reference or transaction number shown on the receipt in your e-wallet or banking app after you send your donation. We use it to match your payment to your details.
Which projects can I donate to?
Donations support our ongoing flagship projects: Sibol Katutubo, Sinag Lingap, and Guhit Pangarap. These show a donate button when you open them on the home page.
Can I get a refund?
Donations are generally not refundable, but we will help if something went wrong with your payment. See our Refund Policy.
